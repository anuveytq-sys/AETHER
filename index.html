PRIVACY & SAFETY CENTER

Your data. Your decisions.

  • AETHER stores this workspace in this browser’s local storage; it is not cloud-synced or encrypted cloud storage.
  • Calls, WhatsApp messages, email drafts, and video calls require your review and action in the relevant device app.
  • AETHER does not silently monitor contacts, track people, or send messages in the background.
  • Only import contact files and share support information when you have permission.
  • Export a backup before clearing browser data; keep exported files private.
